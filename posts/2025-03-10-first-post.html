First Post

This is the first post of this blog and the first blog post i have ever written. As I am pressing the buttons on my keyboard, I see the possible continuations for my sentences next to what I have already written. I am not schizophrenic, I am just using copilot to write the html pages. And this brings us to one of the motivations behind this blog. The rapid development of large language models and their consequences have been a disaster for the internet. It also was detrimental for my personal skills and capabilities of writing in general (code and otherwise). This blog is here for me to practice writing and for the internet to have some human generated content.

The second reason of existence of this blog is for me to gain some front-end web development experience. I am currently working on multimedia streaming and media over QUIC. As closely related as these things are to WWW, the work rarely necessiates me to write webpages. And when it does I can usually get by with some ChatGPT generated html. But having that very surface level experience doesn't tell me why we need all those javascript frameworks. So I want to suffer a little using pure HTML enough to justify all those frameworks. I might keep increasing the scope of this blog to introduce more and more suffering for that.

The final reason for this blog is to have some creative outlet to deal with PhD. When I say it like that it sounds like PhD is some sort of a mental condition (it feels like so). I have some frustrations with the path I chose and my personal experience with academia. I think it would be beneficial to me and maybe even for others to write about my experiences. So I can look back and reflect or others can learn from my mistakes. I'm hoping that my professor won't find this blog :)

And hocam, if you did, please do not think this as me listing my reasons to quit the program. On the contrary, I am trying to find ways to resolve my frustrations and better focus on my studies.
